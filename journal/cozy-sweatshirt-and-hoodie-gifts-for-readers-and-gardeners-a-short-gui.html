Before you buy: timing, honestly

Our sweatshirts and hoodies are made to order by our print partner. That means production takes about 2-7 business days before the item even ships, and shipping time comes on top of that. We can't promise it will arrive by Christmas. If the date matters, check the estimated delivery date Etsy shows on the listing for your location, order early, and consider a small backup gift (a printable card or tag you can print tonight works well).

Not-quite-in-time is also fine for many people: a "your gift is on its way" note and a cozy sweatshirt that arrives in the first days of January still lands as a good present.

How to choose size and color

  • Check the size chart on the listing. These are unisex cuts, and the fit is relaxed. If you are unsure, ask what size they usually wear in a favorite sweatshirt, or measure one they already own.
  • Between sizes? The relaxed fit means most people can size up for a roomier, layered look or stay true to size for a neater one.
  • Pick the color that suits the artwork. Light and natural colors, such as white, cream and ash, show our designs best in the mockup photos.
  • Think about how they will wear it. A hoodie with a front pouch pocket suits someone who is in and out of the garden or the yard. A crewneck is easier under a coat and is the usual pick for a reading chair.
