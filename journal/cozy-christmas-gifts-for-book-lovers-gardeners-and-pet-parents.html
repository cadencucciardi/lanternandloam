Start with the person, not the product

Sizing, materials and care

  • Mugs: 11 oz ceramic, with the artwork on one side. Hand wash them for the longest life of the print.
  • Tees: unisex heavyweight. Check the size chart before ordering. Machine wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low.
  • Colors: most of these designs use dark brown type and line work, so they read best on light or natural blanks such as white, cream, natural or sand.
  • Printables: nothing ships. You receive the PDF by download and print it yourself. Use a printer you trust and check the page size is set to US Letter or A4 before you print.

Timing your order

Physical items are made to order. Each one takes 2 to 7 business days to produce before it ships, and shipping time comes on top of that. Count back from the date you need the gift, and check the delivery estimate at checkout before you buy. If a printable is the safer choice for a tight deadline, it is available right away.

Replacements and refunds are offered only for damaged or misprinted items, so please look at the product photos and size chart before you order.

Simple ways to wrap a gift

  • Tuck a printed journal inside a card with a paperback you already love.
  • Pair a mug with a bag of loose tea, a packet of seeds or a favorite local treat.
  • Set a tee on top of a small box of coffee or tea for a hiker or camper.
  • For a dog walker or pet sitter, add a handwritten note and a small treat for their pet.

Gifts do not need to be expensive to feel thoughtful. Choose the person's habit, pick one piece that fits it, and write a line by hand. That is usually the part people remember.
