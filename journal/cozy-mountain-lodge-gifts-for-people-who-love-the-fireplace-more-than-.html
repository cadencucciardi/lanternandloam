Cozy Mountain Lodge Gifts for People Who Love the Fireplace More Than the Slopes

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Every group has one: the person who plans the mountain trip for the lodge, not the lift line. They want the stone fireplace, the wool socks, a hot drink in both hands and a long morning with nothing scheduled. If that is someone on your gift list, this guide is for you. Everything below is an item from our shop that is live today.

Start with the hot drink

A lodge weekend runs on mugs. A mug is also the easiest gift to get right, because it gets used the same day it is unwrapped.

  • Tea First, Then the Mountain mug: a sunrise arch with layered peaks over a steaming mug. Good for the tea drinker who hikes, skis or just likes to look at mountains from a window.
  • Cocoa Weather mug: a glowing cocoa mug with marshmallows in a winter arch. This one suits kids and grown-ups who treat cocoa as a serious activity.

Tip: put the mug in a gift bag with a few tea bags or a cocoa mix you chose yourself. That turns one item into a small gift set for about the price of a coffee.

Something warm to wear by the fire

Sweatshirts are the classic lodge-night item: easy to pull on over base layers, comfortable to sit in for hours.

  • Tea First, Then the Mountain sweatshirt: the same sunrise-peaks art as the mug, so you can pair them as a set.
  • Slow Mornings, Long Trails sweatshirt: a stitched-patch sunrise over layered ridges with a steaming cup. A fit for hikers, campers and anyone whose best part of a trip is the first hour.
  • Home Is Wherever the Kettle Is sweatshirt: a night camp scene with a glowing tent, lantern and kettle.

Sizing and care: these are printed to order on Printify blanks. Check the size chart on each listing and size up if the recipient likes a roomy fit over layers.

A bag for the whole trip

Totes do more work on a mountain weekend than you would think: books, snacks, a thermos, wet gloves.

  • Tea First, Then the Mountain tote
  • Slow Mornings, Long Trails tote
  • Home Is Wherever the Kettle Is tote

For the kids on the trip

If the trip includes children, a rainy or snowed-in afternoon is where printables earn their keep. These download instantly, so they work even as a last-minute gift:

  • Christmas Puzzle Pages for Kids
  • Cozy Christmas Coloring Pages for Kids

Print a set before you leave and tuck it in the car or the cabin bag.

Build a simple lodge-night gift set

1. Pick one wearable (sweatshirt) or one mug. 2. Add something to drink: tea, cocoa or cider mix. 3. Add something to do: a paperback, a deck of cards or a printable puzzle pack. 4. Wrap it in the tote, so the wrapping is part of the gift.

Order timing

Our POD items are made to order and need a few business days to produce before shipping, so order early if you need them by a specific date. Instant-download printables are the fallback for last-minute gifts.
