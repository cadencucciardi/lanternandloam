Last-Minute Neighbor, Coworker and Teacher Gifts You Can Print Tonight

Some people on your list do not need a big present. They need a small, warm gesture that says "thinking of you." Neighbors, coworkers, bus drivers, teachers: the goal is thoughtful, quick and inexpensive. Here is a plan that works even if it is already late in the evening.

The formula: something small + something handwritten

A good last-minute gift has two parts: a small treat (cocoa, tea, cookies, a candle, a gift card) and a personal touch (a tag, a note). The personal touch is what makes a $5 gift feel considered. And it is the part you can print at home tonight.

Gift ideas you can make tonight

  • Cocoa or tea jar: fill a jar or paper bag with cocoa mix, tea bags or cookies, and tie on a tag.
  • Coffee shop or bookstore gift card: slip it in a plain envelope and decorate with a tag.
  • Baked goods plate: cookies on a paper plate wrapped in parchment, with a handwritten tag.
  • A favorite book plus a tea bag: a classic for readers.
  • Candle or hand cream: pair with a short note about why you appreciate them.

A quick tonight checklist

  • Pick one small treat per person.
  • Print the gift tags on cardstock if you have it.
  • Cut, punch, tie with twine or ribbon.
  • Write a specific line of thanks on each one. Specific beats fancy.
  • Deliver within a day or two, or tuck it in a bag at the door.

A note on budgets

Neighbor and coworker gifts do not need to cost much. A jar of cocoa with a printed tag, made for a few dollars each, often lands better than something expensive and generic. Teachers especially tend to remember a kind, specific note.
