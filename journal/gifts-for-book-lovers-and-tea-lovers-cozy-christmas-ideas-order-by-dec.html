Gifts for Book Lovers and Tea Lovers: Cozy Christmas Ideas (Order by Dec 5, Plus Instant Printables)

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

If someone on your list lives with a book in one hand and a hot cup in the other, you already know the gift: something that makes that ritual feel a little more special. This guide pulls together cozy ideas for book lovers and tea lovers, with a clear note on timing, because printed gifts are made to order and shipping dates matter.

First, the dates

Our mugs, ornaments and sweatshirts are printed to order by our production partner, Printify, so they are not in a warehouse waiting to ship. Production plus shipping takes time, and December is busy for every carrier.

  • Order by Dec 5 for Christmas delivery (US). That is the date we are comfortable recommending. It is not a guarantee, since carriers can run late in peak season.
  • After Dec 5, switch to instant downloads. A printable arrives in minutes, and you can wrap it tonight.

For someone who is both

Pair a mug with a paperback and a box of loose-leaf tea you chose yourself. A mug from this guide plus a book you loved is a gift that feels personal without costing much. If the budget is tight, an ornament and a handwritten note go a long way.

After Dec 5: gifts you can send in minutes

Once the printed-gift window closes, instant downloads are the honest way to keep giving.

  • Lantern Light Reading Journal (printable): a printable book log for readers who like to keep track of what they have read and loved. You download it, print it at home or at a print shop, and tuck it into a card or folder.

How to give a printable so it feels like a real gift:

  • Print it on slightly heavier paper if your printer allows.
  • Punch holes and put it in a small binder, or fold it into a card.
  • Add a pen and a bookmark.
  • Write a note on the first page naming the first book you hope they log.

Please note that a printable is a digital file. Nothing is shipped to you, and it is meant for personal use.

A simple gift plan by date

  • Now through Dec 5: order a mug, ornament or sweatshirt.
  • Dec 6 onward: choose an instant download and print it yourself.
  • Dec 24: a printable is still possible, but you will need printer time and paper on hand.
