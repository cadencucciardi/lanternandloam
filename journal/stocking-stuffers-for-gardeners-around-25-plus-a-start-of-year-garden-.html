How to use a garden planner in the first weeks of the year:

  • List what worked and what didn't. Last year's notes are the best guide to this year's plan.
  • Sketch your beds. A rough drawing is enough. Mark sunny and shady spots.
  • Choose a few things to grow. A short list beats an overwhelming one.
  • Check your local frost dates. Your regional extension service is a reliable source. Then work backward to plan when to start seeds.
  • Keep the page where you'll see it. A fridge or a potting bench works well.

To give it as a present, print it, slip it into a folder with a seed catalog, and add a pencil.
