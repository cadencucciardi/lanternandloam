Last-Week Gift Picks for Readers, Tea Lovers and Slow-Morning People (Dec 2-24)

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Some people on your list never ask for anything. They want a quiet hour, a hot cup and a book that is not on a screen. Gifts for them do not need to be fancy; they need to fit that small, cozy routine. This guide gathers a handful of our own designs that suit readers, tea lovers and slow-morning people, with straight talk about timing. We are running a sale from December 2 to December 24: 10% off hoodies, sweatshirts and printables, and 5% off tees, totes and mugs. Stickers are not part of the sale.

Timing first, honestly

Everything we sell in physical form is made to order by our print partner. Production takes about 2-7 business days, and shipping comes on top of that. We cannot promise delivery by Christmas. Before you buy, look at the estimated delivery date Etsy shows on the listing for your address. If the date is tight, do two things: order early, and pair the gift with something you can print at home tonight, such as the printable below. A "your gift is on its way" note is a perfectly good present on Christmas morning.
