Printable Christmas Fun You Can Print Tonight

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

It is the last stretch before Christmas and the list still has gaps: something for the kids to do on a long afternoon, something special for Christmas Eve, and a pile of gifts with nothing to label them. Printables are a good fit for exactly this. There is no shipping and no waiting. You download a PDF, print it on the paper you already own, and you are done in an evening.

Here is a simple plan using three kits from our shop, with honest notes on what each one is and isn't.

Before you start: what you need

  • A home printer (or a nearby print shop or library that prints for a small fee)
  • Plain white paper for most pages, and a few sheets of cardstock if you want sturdier tags and cards
  • Scissors, and a pencil or crayons for the kids' pages
  • About 30 minutes for printing and cutting the first kit

Every kit below is an instant digital download. Nothing is mailed to you, and files are sized for both US Letter and A4 paper.

For a long afternoon with kids: Kids Christmas Activity Pack

The Kids Christmas Activity Pack is a 16-page printable with coloring pages, mazes, a word search and a letter to Santa. It is made for the stretch between school letting out and the big day, when children have lots of energy and the adults still have wrapping to do.

How to use it:

  • Print the whole pack once and keep the pages in a folder, handing out one or two at a time
  • Put out crayons and a mug of cocoa and let the coloring pages do the work
  • Save the letter to Santa for a quiet moment, so the kids can write or draw what they hope for
  • Print a second copy of favorite pages if siblings both want to color the same one

It is a screen-free option, not a toy or a gift on its own. Think of it as a calm hour you can set up in ten minutes.

For Christmas Eve: Christmas Eve Box Kit

The Christmas Eve Box Kit with Reading Nook Bookmarks gives you printable pieces for a Christmas Eve box tradition: bookmarks, a box label, a checklist and a note card. You supply the box and what goes in it.

A simple way to put it together:

  • Pick a box, basket or gift bag and attach the printed label
  • Fill it with a new book, a pair of cozy socks, a small treat and hot chocolate mix
  • Slip a bookmark into the book and tuck in the note card with a short handwritten message
  • Use the checklist so nothing is forgotten before bedtime

The contents are your choice, so you can keep it as small or as generous as you like. The kit only provides the printed parts.

For the pile of presents: Cozy Christmas Gift Tags

The Cozy Christmas Gift Tags set has 24 tags in 4 designs, with cut lines to make trimming easy. Printing on cardstock gives you tags that hold a loop of string well, but regular paper works if that is what you have.

Quick tips:

  • Print the sheet, cut along the lines, and punch a hole in each tag
  • Write names on the tags before attaching them, so you do not mix up gifts
  • Use leftover tags on treats, jars of cookies or the Christmas Eve box

A simple evening plan

If you have one evening, here is an order that works:

  • First 10 minutes: buy and download the kits you want
  • Next 20 minutes: print the gift tags and the Christmas Eve box pieces
  • Next 15 minutes: cut tags and fill the box while something warm is on the stove
  • Last 5 minutes: print two or three activity pages for tomorrow and put them by the crayons

A note on what we are not promising

Printables are digital files. You will print and assemble them yourself, and results depend on your printer and paper. We cannot promise a particular look on every printer. If a file does not open or something looks off, message the shop and we will help.
