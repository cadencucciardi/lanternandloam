It is late November, which is the sweet spot for "I should sort the gifts" panic. This guide is for the person who has a gardener, a reader and a pet person on the list and wants a plan that is easy and honest about timing. We sort gifts by what you can have today (instant downloads) and what needs to be made and shipped (printed items).

First, an honest word about timing

Our shirts, mugs, totes and sweatshirts are made to order by our production partner, Printify. That means 2-7 business days of processing before shipping, and then transit.

Our advice: order printed gifts by Dec 7 for US Christmas delivery. That date comes from Printify's suggested order-by dates for standard shipping to arrive by Dec 24. It is a guide, not a guarantee, because weather and carrier delays happen. Check the delivery estimate on the listing page before you buy.

Printables have no such problem. They download the moment you pay, so after Dec 7, choose instant downloads.
