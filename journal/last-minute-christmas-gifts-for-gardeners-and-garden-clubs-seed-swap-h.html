How to run a simple seed swap (a quick starter)

If you are giving a host gift, you may be giving it to someone who is planning the next one. Here is the short version:

  • Pick a date in late winter, when people are itching to plant.
  • Ask each guest to bring labeled seeds in small envelopes, with the plant name and year saved.
  • Set out a table for seeds and a table for cuttings and bulbs.
  • Provide blank labels, pens and a sign-up sheet for email.
  • Keep it low-pressure: take some, leave some.
