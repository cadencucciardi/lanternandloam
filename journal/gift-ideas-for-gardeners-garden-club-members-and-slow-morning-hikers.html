Gift Ideas for Gardeners, Garden Club Members and Slow-Morning Hikers

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Gardeners are some of the easiest people to shop for once you stop looking for the perfect trowel. They already own the tools. What they rarely buy for themselves is something that says "this is my thing": a tote for the plant sale, a mug for the first cold morning in the greenhouse, a sweatshirt for pulling on over muddy knees. This guide covers gifts for gardeners and garden club members, plus two ideas for the camper or hiker who is also on your list.

How to choose a gift for a gardener

Start with how they actually garden, not with the plants.

  • They go to meetings, swaps and plant sales: a tote bag is the most used gift of all. It carries seed packets, cuttings and the casserole.
  • They garden early, in the cold: a mug or a warm layer wins.
  • They are new to gardening: go for something wearable or displayable, and keep tools for later.
  • They already have everything: pick something personal to their club or their identity as a gardener rather than another gadget.

Easy gift bundles for gardeners

Pair one item with something small and useful and it feels like a thought-out gift rather than a single product.

  • The plant swap host: the tote plus a few seed packets and a pair of garden gloves.
  • The cold-morning gardener: the mug plus a tin of loose tea or a bag of good coffee.
  • The beginner: the tee or sweatshirt plus a small potted herb.
  • The club secretary or the one who organizes everything: the tote with a notebook and a pen inside.

Think about who is receiving the gift. A club that meets monthly will likely appreciate a shared gift like a set of stickers for everyone more than one big present.

Tips for buying print-on-demand gifts

  • Check sizing. Look at the size chart on the listing before ordering a shirt or sweatshirt, and size up if the person likes a relaxed fit.
  • Order early. Made-to-order items need a few days to produce and then time to ship.
  • Ship straight to the recipient if you will not see them in person, and add a note at checkout.
  • Think about the set. If a whole club or family is getting gifts, matching items can be a fun touch.

A quick note on budget

Stickers and mugs are the most affordable options, totes and tees sit in the middle, and sweatshirts and hoodies are the larger gifts. If you are shopping for several people, a mix works well: a sweatshirt for the closest person, a tote for the club member, a sticker for everyone else.
