How to make a printable feel like a real gift

  • Print on slightly heavier paper if your printer allows it.
  • Pair it with something small: a pen, a tea bag, a candle.
  • Add a handwritten note and the recipient's name.
  • Use the gift tags to finish the wrap.

It takes about 15 minutes, and nobody needs to know you started on Dec 23.
