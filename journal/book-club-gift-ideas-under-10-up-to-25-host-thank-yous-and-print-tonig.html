Under $10: a small something for every member

For a group of eight or ten, you want something thoughtful that doesn't cost much. A few ideas that work:

  • Bookmarks. Handmade, or print and cut your own. Write a line from the year's reading on the back.
  • A tea bag or hot-cocoa sachet tied to a bookmark with string. Book club and a warm drink go together.
  • A printed reading-journal page. Our Lantern Light Reading Journal printable is a downloadable book log. Print a few pages for each member, staple them with a cover, and you have a gift for the cost of paper. (It's also on Gumroad if you prefer.)
  • A pencil and a small pack of sticky tabs for marking favorite passages.

Wrap them in brown paper and add a tag. Our Cozy Christmas Gift Tags printable has 24 tags in four designs, which is enough for a whole club with spares. You print them at home and cut them out.

Up to $25: the book club gift exchange

A swap is the most fun way to end the year, and a firm price cap keeps it fair. Twenty-five dollars is a good ceiling; fifteen works for larger groups.

How to run it simply:

  • Agree on the cap and the date at least three weeks ahead.
  • Draw names on paper slips, or do a white elephant swap where people pick and steal.
  • Ask everyone to write one line about their taste (favorite genre, favorite drink, allergies) so gifts fit.
  • Wrap everything in a book-themed way: newspaper, twine, a tag.
  • Use the same signature move every year. Our favorite: each person names their best read of the year as they unwrap.

Thank-you gifts for your host

Whoever hosts your club gives up their living room, makes snacks and washes the cups afterward. A hostess gift doesn't need to be large. It just needs to say you noticed.

  • A mug and a tea bag. Simple, useful, and it makes a nice thank-you from the whole club if everyone chips in a few dollars.
  • A tote for the host to carry the next meeting's book.
  • A handwritten card signed by everyone. Free, and often the one people keep.
  • A sweatshirt for a club that wants a bigger gift. The Merry & Booked sweatshirt is a cozy winter option. Order by December 7 for Christmas delivery.

If the host has been hosting all year, split the cost among members. Everyone pays a little, and the host gets something nicer.

A year-end ritual worth keeping

At your last meeting of the year, have each member name their favorite read of the year and one book they gave up on. It takes ten minutes, and you'll walk out with next year's reading list. It's also a good moment to hand out whichever small gift you chose.
