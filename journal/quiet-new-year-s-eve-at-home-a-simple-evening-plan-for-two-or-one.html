Quiet New Year's Eve at Home: A Simple Evening Plan for Two (or One)

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Not everyone wants a crowd on December 31. If your ideal night is a good meal, a warm blanket and a clear head for January, here is a quiet plan you can do alone or with a partner or friend. Pick the parts you like and skip the rest.

Set the evening up (5 to 7 pm)

Keep the food easy so you are not stuck in the kitchen. A cheese and fruit board, soup and good bread, or breakfast for dinner all work. Turn off the overhead lights and use lamps or candles. Put your phone in another room for the first hour.

Look back at the year

Take 15 minutes with a pen and paper and answer three questions:

  • What am I proud of this year?
  • What was harder than I expected?
  • What do I want to carry into next year, and what can I leave?

Plan the first weeks of January

A fresh start feels better when the first step is small. Choose one area to reset, such as the kitchen or the entryway, and write down when you will do it. Our Slow Home Reset Cleaning Planner is a printable cleaning schedule you can print tonight and start tomorrow.

Write down a few intentions

Skip the long resolution list. Write three words or three small habits, and put the page where you will see it. Then toast with whatever you like: sparkling cider, tea or a glass of wine.
