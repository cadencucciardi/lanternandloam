The reader in your life already owns more books than shelves, and the tea drinker has a cupboard of mugs. The best gifts for both are small, cozy and a little bit "that's so you". Here is how to pick one without a lot of scrolling, with ordering dates that match how print-on-demand really works.

Know the dates first

Our mugs, ornaments and sweatshirts are printed to order by our production partner, Printify, so they are not instant. Our shop copy says to order by Dec 5 for Christmas delivery in the US. After that, delivery by Dec 25 is not something we can promise, so treat anything ordered later as a post-Christmas gift or a "your gift is on its way" card.

Printables are different. They are instant digital downloads with no shipping, and you can print them tonight on your own printer. That makes them the true last-minute option, all the way through Dec 24.
