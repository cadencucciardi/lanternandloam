Last-Minute Christmas Gifts for Teachers and Coworkers (Printable Thank-Yous Plus Mugs and Ornaments)

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Every December the same thing happens. The class list goes home, the office Secret Santa draw is announced, and you realize you need something for a teacher, a bus driver or the person who sits next to you. This guide sorts the options by one question: how much time do you have?

First, the honest delivery rules

Two kinds of gifts, two different clocks:

  • Printables are instant. You buy, download the PDF and print at home. Nothing ships, so these work right up to the last few days before Christmas.
  • Physical items are made to order. Our mugs and ornaments are printed on demand by our production partner, Printify. Production takes 2-7 business days, then US shipping usually takes about 3-7 business days. To give them the best chance of arriving by Dec 25, order by Dec 10. We can't guarantee carrier delivery dates, and a mug and an ornament may arrive in separate parcels.

If it's already past Dec 10 when you read this, skip to the printable section. It's the safest route.

Gifts you can finish tonight (printables)

A handwritten note is the thing teachers say they keep. A printable lets you add one to almost any small gift without a trip to the store.

  • Cozy Christmas Gift Tags (24 tags, 4 designs). Print them on regular paper or cardstock, cut them out and tie them onto a coffee gift card, a jar of cocoa mix or a plate of cookies. A tag with a real name and a real sentence makes a $5 gift look thought through. See the gift tags

A tip that works for teachers and coworkers alike: write one specific sentence on the tag. "Thank you for being so patient with the reading group" lands better than "Merry Christmas."

A simple plan by budget

  • Under $5: a printable gift tag, a handwritten line and a small treat you already have.
  • Around $20: one mug or ornament plus a tea bag or cocoa packet, with a printed tag.
  • For a group: pool the class or team, give one mug with a gift card inside and a card everybody signs.

Who not to forget

Teachers get the most attention, but the following people often don't:

  • Bus drivers and crossing guards
  • Coaches and music or art teachers
  • Daycare and after-school staff
  • The coworker who covered your shift

A tag and a short note costs almost nothing and is remembered.

Quick checklist

  • Decide if the gift ships or prints. After Dec 10, choose a printable or something you can buy locally.
  • Write one specific thank-you sentence for each person.
  • Print extra tags in case of mistakes. A 24-tag sheet covers a whole class helper list.
  • Keep it small. Teachers and coworkers rarely want more clutter, and a useful mug or a keepsake ornament lands better than a large gift.

Gift giving at this time of year is more about being remembered than spending a lot. Choose what fits your time, and write the note.
