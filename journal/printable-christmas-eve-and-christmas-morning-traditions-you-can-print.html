Printable Christmas Eve and Christmas Morning Traditions You Can Print Tonight

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Christmas is about a month away, and the best traditions are usually small ones: a quiet hour on Christmas Eve, a tidy stack of wrapped gifts, a morning that starts slowly. The good news is that you don't need to order anything or wait for a package. Instant-download printables arrive in minutes, and you can print them at home on regular paper or card stock.

Here is how we would build a simple Christmas Eve and morning routine around printables, plus a few ideas for the people on your list.

Why printables suit last-minute traditions

Shipped gifts have cut-off dates. Printables don't. You buy, download, print and use them the same evening. That makes them handy for:

  • Traditions you decide to start at the last minute
  • Gifts for people who are hard to shop for
  • Small extras for stockings, cards and gift baskets
  • Families who like a hands-on activity on Christmas Eve

A practical note: download your files in a web browser rather than the Etsy app, which can make PDFs harder to find. The files are print-and-write PDFs in US Letter and A4 sizes.

1. Wrap gifts with printable gift tags

Gift tags are the quickest way to make a pile of presents feel finished. Print on card stock, cut them out, punch a hole and tie them on with string or ribbon.

Our Cozy Christmas Gift Tags set has 24 tags in 4 designs, so you can mix and match across a whole tree's worth of gifts. Tip: write the names in pencil first, then pen over them, so a slip doesn't waste a tag.

A Christmas Eve idea: turn tag-writing into a ritual. Put on a record, pour something warm, and have everyone write one line on a tag for someone else, such as a favorite memory from the year.

2. Make a Christmas Eve reading night

If your family loves books, a reading night is one of the easiest traditions to start. Everyone picks a book (or one chapter), gets a blanket and a warm drink, and reads for a set time before bed.

The Lantern Light Reading Journal gives the reader in your life a place to log what they finish, so a Christmas Eve book becomes the first entry of the new year. It also works as a no-fuss gift: print the pages, punch them and put them in a simple binder with a pen.

For kids, keep it light. Let them draw a picture of their favorite page instead of writing a review.

3. Give a gardener a plan to dream over

Winter is when gardeners start daydreaming about spring. A printable garden planner makes a thoughtful gift because it gives them something to do during the dark months.

Our Small Plot, Big Plans garden planner is a one-page layout printable in US Letter, good for sketching beds, pots or a balcony. Pair it with a packet of seeds and a pencil, wrapped with one of the gift tags above.

4. Add a cozy wall print to the room

If you like to decorate for the season, our printable wall art, "Light a Lantern, Stay a While", is an 8x10 print you can frame for the mantel or shelf. You can find it in the shop. It suits a slow, warm Christmas morning rather than a loud one.

A simple Christmas Eve and morning plan

Here is one way to put it together without much effort:

  • Afternoon: Print the tags and wrap gifts while the kitchen is quiet.
  • Evening: Hold a tag-writing hour with warm drinks.
  • Bedtime: Read one chapter each, with the reading journal handy.
  • Morning: Start slowly. Coffee or tea first, then gifts in small turns so everyone sees each one.
  • Afterwards: Note one favorite moment from the day, so you can look back next year.

Quick tips for printing at home

  • Use card stock (65-110 lb) for tags and anything that will be handled.
  • Print a test page first to check sizing.
  • Choose "actual size" rather than "fit to page" so tags come out the right size.
  • If you don't have a printer, save the PDF to a USB stick and use a local print shop.

Small rituals add up. Pick one or two from this list, and let the rest go.
