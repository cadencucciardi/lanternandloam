Last-Minute Christmas Gifts You Can Print Tonight

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Whether it is early December or Christmas Eve, printables are the gift you can have in hand tonight. You download the file, print it at home or at a local print shop, and have something real to wrap the same evening. Nothing ships, so nothing can arrive late.

This guide covers six printables from our shop, who each one suits, and a few practical tips so they look good on paper. All are instant digital downloads in a single PDF with US Letter and A4 pages.

Before you print: a few quick tips

  • Print at "Actual Size" or 100%, not "fit to page", so cut lines and labels come out the right size.
  • White cardstock (about 65-110 lb) gives bookmarks, tags and cards a sturdier feel. Plain paper works for cards you will fill in.
  • Colors look a little different on every screen and printer. Do a test print of one page first.
  • Printables are for personal use: print as many copies as you like for your own family and gifts, but do not resell or share the file.

A simple last-minute gift plan

  • Pick one printable that matches the person: the Eve box for a family, the planner for a host, the journal for a reader.
  • Print on cardstock, trim, and add a small something: a pen, tea bags, a bookmark ribbon.
  • Wrap it with a printed tag.
  • Write one handwritten sentence on the card or tag explaining why you chose it.

That last step costs nothing, and it is usually the part people remember.

Gifting tips for printables

  • Give the printed pages in a folder, envelope or small box rather than handing over loose sheets.
  • If the gift is the file itself, tell the person it is a digital download, so they know they can print more copies for personal use.
  • Check which paper size you need: each PDF includes both US Letter and A4 pages.

Whatever you choose, a few printed pages and a bit of thought go a long way when time is short. Happy wrapping.
