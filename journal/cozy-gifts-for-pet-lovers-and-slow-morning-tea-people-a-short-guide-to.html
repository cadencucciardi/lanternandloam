Cozy Gifts for Pet Lovers and Slow-Morning Tea People: A Short Guide to Mugs, Totes and Tees

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

Some people on your list are easy to shop for once you know the one thing they love. This guide covers a few of them: the person whose pet is basically family, the one who won't talk before the first cup of tea, the one who is happiest outside with a kettle, and the one with dirt under their nails. Everything below is a Lantern & Loam original design. Each one is printed to order, so read the ship-by note first.

Ship-by note (please read first)

Printed-to-order items (mugs, tees, totes, sweatshirts, hoodies) are made after you order. For US Christmas delivery, we suggest ordering by Dec 7. After that, the gift may still be lovely, but it may arrive after Christmas, so consider giving it as a "your gift is on its way" present. Printable items are instant downloads, so they work any day, including Christmas Eve.

For the dog person

Best Part of My Day Has Paws is a groovy retro paw-heart design. It comes in five products:

  • Mug: a small, easy gift for a coffee or tea drinker.
  • Tee: for the person who greets the dog before anyone else.
  • Tote: handy for dog-walk errands, library runs and market trips.
  • Sweatshirt: the cozy pick if you want the gift to feel bigger.
  • Hoodie: the same design, with a hood.

For the cat person

Nine Lives, One Favorite Human pairs a green cat holding a rose heart with a moon arch. It comes as a mug, a tee and a tote. The mug is a sweet thing to give someone whose cat decides when the day begins.

For the tea-first, slow-morning person

If the person you're shopping for needs tea and a quiet ten minutes before anything else, these two fit well.

  • Tea First, Then the Mountain tote: layered peaks over a steaming mug. It's a good match for a hiker, a walker or anyone who carries a thermos everywhere.
  • Tea First, Then the Mountain mug: the same design for the desk or the kitchen counter.

For the camper (or anyone who loves a kettle)

Home Is Wherever the Kettle Is shows a glowing tent, lantern and steaming kettle at night. The tee is the easy pick for someone who loves camp mornings, and the tote is a lower-cost way to give the same design.

For the gardener

The Garden Club tote carries the stitched-patch "dirty hands, happy heart" design. It's a safe gift for anyone in a garden club, or anyone who comes in from the yard with muddy knees. A Garden Club tee and mug are also available.

Sales and budget

From Dec 2 to Dec 24, our shop runs a 10% sale on hoodies, sweatshirts and printables, and a 5% sale on tees, totes and mugs. Stickers aren't included. Check the listing for the current price before you buy.

Quick picks by budget

  • Smallest gift: a tote or a mug.
  • Middle: a tee in the design they'd choose.
  • Biggest: a sweatshirt or hoodie, such as Best Part of My Day Has Paws for the dog person.
  • Last-minute after Dec 7: an instant-download printable from the shop. It isn't the same as a mug, but it arrives in minutes.

If you're unsure about sizes, pick a mug or tote. They don't depend on fit, which makes them the easiest to get right.
