Last-minute Christmas gifts you can download today

This guide features products from our own shop, Lantern & Loam. Our designs are created with the help of AI design tools and printed to order by our production partner, Printify.

It's the last stretch before Christmas, and the gift list still has a gap or two. Maybe a coworker you forgot, a teacher, a friend you decided to surprise. Shipping deadlines are closing, but instant downloads don't have a deadline. You buy, download, print at home (or at a local print shop), and wrap. Here is how to make a printable feel like a real gift.

Why printables work for late gifts

A printable is a file, so there is nothing to wait for. Delivery is instant after checkout. The trick is to turn the file into something you can hold: good paper, a pen, a small treat, a ribbon.

  • Print on heavier paper (28-32 lb or light cardstock) so it feels substantial.
  • Pair it with a small, cheap extra, such as a pen, a tea bag or a bookmark.
  • Write a short handwritten note on the first page. It makes a printed page personal.
  • Not sure about your printer? A local copy shop can print on cardstock for a small fee.

Gift tags: finish the wrapping in minutes

Even store-bought gifts look more thoughtful with a tag that matches the paper. The Cozy Christmas Gift Tags (24 tags, 4 designs) set gives you 24 tags across four designs. Print, cut and tie them on with twine. Because it's a download, you can reprint if you run out mid-wrapping.

Also nice: write a tiny message on the back of each tag, like "open this one first."

For the book lover: a reading journal

If someone on your list always has a book on the nightstand, a printable reading journal is an easy win. The Lantern Light Reading Journal includes a book log, a 52-book reading challenge tracker and review pages. Print it, punch holes, and slip it into a binder, or staple the pages into a small booklet.

Wrap it with a bookmark and a pen. If you're new to the idea, our guide on how to start a reading journal can help the recipient get going.

For the person who needs a calmer home: a cleaning planner

This is a good gift for a new homeowner, a busy parent, or a friend who says their house is "always a bit much." The Slow Home Reset Cleaning Planner is built around low-energy days, with weekly checklists and a reset routine instead of guilt. Tuck it in a basket with a nice candle or a set of dish cloths.

For the freelancer or side-hustler: a budget planner

Money gifts are practical, and a lot of people with uneven income have trouble finding a planner that fits. The Lean Month, Full Month Budget Planner is made for irregular income. It suits freelancers, seasonal workers and anyone starting the new year wanting a fresh approach. Give it with a pen and a note: "for a calmer January."

Quick wrapping ideas that take 10 minutes

  • Roll printed pages and tie them with ribbon and a gift tag.
  • Slip pages into a plain folder or kraft envelope.
  • Put the printable in a small gift bag with a mug or a tea sampler.
  • Email the file instead, with a printed first page in a card. This works well for far-away friends.

A note on timing

Printables are digital files, so nothing ships to you. Order the printing a day or two ahead if you use a print shop. If you want a physical item with our designs, such as a mug or ornament, those are made to order and may not arrive before Christmas, so check the delivery estimate on each listing.
