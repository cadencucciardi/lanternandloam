Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers the Lantern & Loam shop and Lantern and Loam HQ, the private tool we use to run our shop and to share our own products on our Pinterest account (@lanternandloam).

Who uses Lantern and Loam HQ

Lantern and Loam HQ is used only by the Lantern & Loam shop owner to manage the shop's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Pinterest data we access

When the shop owner connects our Pinterest account through Pinterest's official sign-in (OAuth), the app requests permission to read our account name, read and create boards, and read and create Pins. We use this only to create Pins for our own Etsy listings on our own boards and to check that they were posted.

What we store and where

  • The Pinterest access token and refresh token, stored only on the shop owner's own computer, never in the browser, never shared and never logged.
  • The IDs of the boards and Pins the app created, so the same product is not pinned twice.

We never ask for or store Pinterest passwords or session cookies, and we never collect data about other Pinterest users.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Pinterest data is used only for the purpose described above.

Customers

Orders are placed and paid on Etsy and handled under Etsy's Privacy Policy. We receive only what is needed to make and ship your order (name, shipping address and the items ordered), and share it only with our print partner, Printify, to produce and ship the order. We do not use it for anything else.

Deleting data and disconnecting

The shop owner can disconnect Pinterest at any time in Lantern and Loam HQ or in Pinterest settings (Settings → Apps), which deletes the stored tokens. To ask about your data, message us through our Etsy shop.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes, with a new date at the top.
